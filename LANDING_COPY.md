Credex AI Spend Auditor

Stop overspending on AI tools.

Credex helps startups and teams understand where their AI budget is going and identifies opportunities to reduce unnecessary spend across tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini, Windsurf, and more.

What the audit checks

- Overpaid plans
- Unused or excess seats
- Cheaper alternatives
- Team size mismatches
- Duplicate AI tooling spend

How it works

1. Add your AI tools and monthly spend
2. Enter seat count and team size
3. Run the audit
4. Instantly receive savings recommendations

Features

- AI spend analysis
- Personalized optimization suggestions
- Annual savings estimation
- AI-generated audit summary
- Lead capture with Supabase
- Local storage persistence
- Fast and lightweight UI

Example insights

- Downgrade unnecessary enterprise plans
- Reduce unused seats
- Replace expensive tools with lower-cost alternatives
- Consolidate overlapping subscriptions

Built with

- React
- Supabase
- Anthropic API
- LocalStorage
- Vercel

Why Credex

AI tooling costs scale quickly as teams grow. Most startups end up paying for overlapping subscriptions, unused seats, or plans that exceed their actual usage. Credex helps teams optimize their stack before costs become difficult to manage.
