\# User Interviews



\## Interview 1

\*\*Name:\*\* RS (preferred anonymity)

\*\*Role:\*\* Engineering Manager

\*\*Company stage:\*\* Series A, 18 person eng team



\*\*Quotes:\*\*

\- "I literally have no idea what we're paying for Copilot

&nbsp; vs what we actually use"

\- "We signed up for ChatGPT Team and half the team

&nbsp; still uses the free version"

\- "I'd use something like this before our next budget review"



\*\*Most surprising thing they said:\*\*

They had 3 overlapping coding assistants active simultaneously —

Cursor, Copilot, and Codeium — because different engineers

preferred different tools and nobody had consolidated.



\*\*What it changed about my design:\*\*

Added team size vs seats comparison to the audit engine.

The overlap problem is real — multiple tools for same use case.

\## Interview 3

\*\*Name:\*\* PM (preferred anonymity)

\*\*Role:\*\* CTO

\*\*Company stage:\*\* Seed, 8 person team



\*\*Quotes:\*\*

\- "We moved from ChatGPT to Claude mid year and forgot

&nbsp;  to cancel the ChatGPT Team subscription for 2 months"

\- "The audit result URL — I'd post that on X honestly"

\- "The AI summary makes it feel premium, not like a spreadsheet"



\*\*Most surprising thing they said:\*\*

They were paying for Anthropic API direct AND Claude Team

simultaneously for different use cases — $150/mo overlap

they hadn't noticed.



\*\*What it changed about my design:\*\*

flagging when someone pays for both API direct and

a subscription plan for the same provider.
