\# Unit Economics



\## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits at roughly 20-30% margin.

A startup spending $500/mo on AI tools, if converted,

buys ~$400/mo through Credex = $80-120/mo margin.

LTV over 12 months = ~$1,000 per converted customer.

Estimate: one converted lead = $800-1200 LTV.



\## CAC per channel

\- HN Show HN post: $0 CAC, ~50 users, conversion 5% = ~$0 CAC

\- Twitter/X viral loop: $0 CAC, reach depends on shareability

\- LinkedIn cold DM: 2 hrs time cost, 10% reply, 2% convert

&nbsp; = roughly $50 CAC in time value per customer

\- Newsletter mention: $0-200 per mention, 1% convert

&nbsp; = $200 CAC worst case



\## Conversion funnel math

Audit completed → email captured: 20% conversion

Email captured → Credex consultation booked: 10%

Consultation booked → credit purchase: 30%

Overall: audit → purchase = 0.6%



\## What makes this $1M ARR in 18 months

Need ~1,000 paying customers at $80/mo margin average.

At 0.6% overall conversion need 167,000 audits completed.

At 200 audits/day (achievable post-HN) = 835 days.

Accelerant: if Credex sales team closes warm leads

from high-saving audits (>$500/mo) directly,

conversion jumps to 15% on that segment.

High-saving audits are maybe 10% of total —

so 16,700 audits → 2,500 customers → $200k ARR.

Combined with long tail: $1M ARR in 18 months is tight but possible.
